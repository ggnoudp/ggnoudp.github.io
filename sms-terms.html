Broadus Consulting Services Text Message Program

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Program Description

Broadus Consulting Services ("we," "us," or "our") offers a text messaging program to keep clients and prospective clients informed about their real estate inquiries. When you opt in, you may receive SMS messages regarding:

  • Responses to your inquiry and consultation scheduling
  • Property showing confirmations and appointment reminders
  • Listing updates and property information you have requested
  • Transaction updates and document reminders
  • Customer care and account-related notifications

2. How to Opt In

You can opt in to receive text messages by checking the SMS consent box on our contact form, by providing your mobile number and verbal or written consent to a member of our team, or by texting a keyword we provide to our business number. By opting in, you confirm that you are the owner or authorized user of the mobile number provided and that you are at least 18 years of age.

Sample message: "Broadus Consulting Services: Hi John, this is a reminder of your property showing tomorrow at 2:00 PM at 123 Main St. Reply YES to confirm. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply."

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your inquiry and the stage of your transaction. You may receive up to 10 messages per month, though most clients receive fewer.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard messaging rates from your wireless carrier will apply to all text messages sent and received. Please contact your wireless carrier for details about your text messaging plan.

5. How to Opt Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Text STOP to any message you receive from us. After you send the message "STOP," we will send you a one-time confirmation message that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. If you want to join again, you can opt in again using any of the methods described above and we will start sending SMS messages to you again.

6. Help and Support

If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program, reply with the keyword HELP for more assistance, or contact us directly at jbroadus@adaxahome.com or (480) 262-5011.

7. Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Our messaging program is supported by all major U.S. carriers, including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and others.

8. Privacy

Your mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information you provide when opting in will be used solely to deliver the messages described above. Please review our Privacy Policy for details on how we collect, use, and protect your information.

9. Changes to These Terms

We may update these SMS Terms from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Your continued participation in the program after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
